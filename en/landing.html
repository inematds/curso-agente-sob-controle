Who it’s for

It’s for you if

  • You have a small business or work for yourself and want to use AI agents without getting burned.
  • You already use an AI chat and have heard about agents that send emails and work with your calendar and spreadsheet.
  • You prefer to learn by doing, with examples from clinics and offices.

It’s not for you if

  • You’re looking for a technical course on systems security.
  • You want a traditional video lesson.
